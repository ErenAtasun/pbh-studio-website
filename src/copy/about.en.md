PBH Studio — short for Primordial Black Hole Studio — is a small independent game studio from Türkiye, founded by Eren Atasun and Gonca Arabacı.

Primordial black holes are thought to have formed in the very first moments of the universe: tiny, ancient and incredibly dense. That is the idea behind the studio. We keep the team small, keep the ideas dense, and try to make games that pull players in.

We love games that make people talk to each other. Our current project, **Pawed Drive**, puts four cats in one car and gives each of them only part of the controls. **Mask Heist** is a multiplayer hide-and-steal game built around masks, traps and one very nervous Hider.
