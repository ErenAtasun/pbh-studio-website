One car, four cats, and only one of you can see the map. **Pawed Drive** is a 1–4 player co-op driving comedy where nobody controls the whole car.

Four cats drive a beat-up car from city to city, taking delivery jobs to earn their food money. One steers, one works the gas and brake, one handles a stubborn manual gearbox, and one sits in the back seat with the phone — the only place the map exists. Talk to each other and the pizza arrives hot. Don't, and the car ends up in a wall.

At the end of every day the cash goes on the garage table. New brakes? A repair? Or a radio nobody asked for? Everyone gets to spend, and there are no vetoes. The map is on the phone. The phone is in the back seat. Good luck.
