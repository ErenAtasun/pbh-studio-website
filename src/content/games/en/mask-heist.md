Every round, one player becomes the **Hider**. They get a random piece of loot and half a minute to stash it somewhere on the map — in a drawer, behind a panel, down in the basement — and set a few traps along the way.

Then the **Seekers** walk in from across the street. Each wears a mask with its own ability, and every mask can turn its wearer invisible for a few seconds. Find the loot, steal it, and the round ends.

The Hider scores for every second the loot stays hidden and every Seeker caught in a trap. The Seekers score for finding it fast. After everyone has had their turn as the Hider, the highest total score wins.
